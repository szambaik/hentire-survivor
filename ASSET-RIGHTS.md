Do not infer image licensing from the wiki footer: [Weird Gloop non-text licensing](https://meta.weirdgloop.org/w/Licensing#Non-text_files).

Stock-asset licensing may be possible for some sprites, but no specific stock pack has been verified here as matching the current game files. A purchase by the game's developer does not transfer rights to this project.

Before monetization, obtain rights covering the exact assets and intended web/PWA/iOS/Android use, including offline caching, public source distribution and the chosen payment/donation model. Base64 is still distribution, not a licensing exemption.
